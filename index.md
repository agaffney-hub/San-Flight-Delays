    <h2>Making sense of flight delays</h2>

    San Diego International Airport operates with a single runway and serves millions of passengers each year. Understanding the factors associated with flight delays can help reveal patterns in airport operations and improve how delay risks are evaluated.

    This project brings together aviation, aircraft, and weather data to examine flight performance and develop predictive models.
